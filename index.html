Who we are

A people with one heart: wholly given to God.

At Grace City Ekklesia, we believe the Church is called to be more than a place we attend. We are a people being shaped by Jesus, growing together, and carrying His presence into our homes, communities, and generation.

Our vision

To see a generation of wholly given men and women established in Christ, transformed by the truth of God's Word and the power of the Holy Spirit, and living as faithful witnesses of His Kingdom.

“
To raise men and women whose hearts are wholly given to God through the faithful teaching of God's Word and the demonstration of the Spirit and power.
